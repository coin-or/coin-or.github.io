COIN-OR has hosted a number of workshops over time. The most recent was in 2018, when COIN-OR was awarded funding by the [IMA](https://www.ima.umn.edu/) (Institute for Mathematics and Its Applications) to run a week-long workshop followed by a series of “coding sprints” to take place over the following year. The workshop and coding sprints are designed to support development of COIN-OR projects and infrastructure, as well as improve the sustainability of COIN-OR projects through improved documentation, testing, and maintenance. Descriptions of sprints are maintained here for archival purposes.

[**Kickoff Workshop (October 2018):**](/ima/oct2018/) The kickoff workshop will take place October 15-19, 2018 and will be hosted by the [IMA](https://www.ima.umn.edu/) in Minneapolis, MN, USA. A draft agenda is now available and we’re seeking feedback, as well as participants. [Read More…](/ima/oct2018/)

**HIGHs LP Solver Coding Sprint (February 2019):** The week of February 18-22, the IMA will host developers of the HIGHs LP solver and others with an interest in integrating this new, high-performance solver engine into the COIN-OR Optimization Suite.

**[MOSDEX Coding Sprint (February 2019):](/feb-2019-mosdex/)** The week of February 25-March 1, the IMA will host developers of MOSDEX, a JSON-based language for model specification and instance data interchange. [Read more…](/feb-2019-mosdex/)
