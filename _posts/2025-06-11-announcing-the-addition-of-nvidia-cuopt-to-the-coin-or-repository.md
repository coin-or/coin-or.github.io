---
title: "Announcing the Addition of NVIDIA cuOpt to the COIN-OR Repository"
date: 2025-06-11 09:00:00
author: Ted Ralphs
---

We are pleased to [announce](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/accelerate-decision-optimization-using-open-source-nvidia-cuopt/) the addition of NVIDIA [cuOpt](https://github.com/coin-or/cuOPT) to the [COIN-OR](/) repository on Github. [cuOpt](https://github.com/coin-or/cuOPT) is an open source library implementing GPU-accelerated algorithms for optimization. The addition of [cuOpt](https://github.com/coin-or/cuOPT) to the [COIN-OR](/) repository follows the announcement of COIN-OR’s collaboration with NVIDIA, which you can read about [here](/2025/03/18/the-coin-or-foundation-bridging-new-frontiers-in-open-source-optimization-with-nvidia/).
