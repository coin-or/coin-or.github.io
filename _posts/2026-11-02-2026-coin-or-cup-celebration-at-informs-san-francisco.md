#### COIN-OR Cup Announcement

The winner of the 2026 COIN-OR Cup will be announced at the INFORMS Computing Society (ICS) Business Meeting on Monday, November 2, at 6:45 pm in Moscone South, Room 215 (Level 2).

#### COIN-OR Cup Celebration

Join us as we celebrate this year's winner of the COIN-OR Cup, the most coveted prize in computational Operations Research. After the business meeting, we will walk over to the nearby [The Chieftain Irish Pub & Restaurant](https://www.thechieftain.com/), 198 5th Street, San Francisco. We plan to be there around 8:30 pm!
