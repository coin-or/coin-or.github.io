#### 2026 COIN-OR Cup Winner: NeuroMANCER

The 2026 COIN-OR Cup Award Committee (Burcin Bozkaya, Haihao Lu and
Stefan Vigerske) has released the following announcement:

We are pleased to announce that the NeuroMANCER team is awarded the
2026 COIN-OR Cup.

The committee has reviewed 7 finalist applications this year on the
basis of originality, quality of open-source project and its code, and
impact. The project NeuroMANCER, an open source project developed by
Prof. Jan Drgona of Johns Hopkins University and his team has earned
this well-deserved award with flying colors.

NeuroMANCER is an open-source PyTorch library that unifies
physics-informed learning, constrained optimization, system
identification, and model-based control in a single differentiable
programming framework. It enables physics-aware and constrained
learning to optimize methods for parametric programming with a
user-friendly syntax inspired by CVXPY, Pyomo, and JuMP.

Team members include:
Aaron Tuor
James Koch
Madelyn Shapiro
Rahul Birmiwal
Bruno Jacob
Draguna Vrabie

We wholeheartedly congratulate Prof. Drgona and the NeuroMANCER team
for this remarkable achievement and wish them success with further
development and deployment of their open source library by the OR
community.
