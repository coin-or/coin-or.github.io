There’s a great overview of software available for optimisation in [Optima (volume 103, 2017)](http://www.mathopt.org/Optima-Issues/optima103.pdf), the Mathematical Optimization Society Newsletter, including the article “A Lightning Tour of the Optimization, Modeling Software Landscape” written by COIN-OR’s chair [Matthew Saltzman](https://mthsc.clemson.edu/directory/view_person.py?person_id=71). This newsletter also includes a good tutorial for COIN-OR’s PuLP, written by its developer (and optimisation guru/consultant) [Stu Mitchell](http://www.stuartmitchell.com/).
