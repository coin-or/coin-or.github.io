---
title: "CBC now available on Google Sheets"
date: 2016-11-07 10:00:00
author: Andrew Mason
---

A new version of [OpenSolver for Google Sheets](http://opensolver.org/) added support for the COIN-OR CBC solver via [NEOS](https://neos-server.org/). This free app allows LP and IP models to be formulated and solved online within Google Sheets. For more details, see [here](http://opensolver.org/opensolver-for-google-sheets/).
