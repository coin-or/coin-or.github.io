---
title: "COIN-OR Google Groups"
---

COIN-OR runs a number of Google discussion groups. These include:

[Coin-Announce](https://groups.google.com/u/2/a/groups.coin-or.org/g/coin-announce) – annoucements of general interest to the Operations Research and COIN-OR user community

[Coin-Discuss](https://groups.google.com/u/2/a/groups.coin-or.org/g/coin-discuss) – General discussion about COIN-OR activities and software

[Coin-Voting-Members](https://groups.google.com/u/2/a/groups.coin-or.org/g/coin-voting-members) – A group for members of COIN-OR used for elections and other similar activities

We also have (or will have soon) discussion groups on Github for each of the COIN-OR projects. For example, the COIN-OR CBC discussion group is at <https://github.com/coin-or/Cbc/discussions>
