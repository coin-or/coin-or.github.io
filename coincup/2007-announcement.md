INFORMS Annual Meeting 2007

Announcing the third annual "COIN-OR INFORMS Cup" competition --- the most coveted prize in computational OR\!

**[Previous Winners](/coincup/)**

**Rules:**

  - A submission or nomination must contain:
    1.  A synopsis of an effective use of COIN-OR or valuable contribution to COIN-OR.
    2.  A cover letter explaining the significance.
  - **The winner must accept the prize in person at the 2007 INFORMS meeting** --- so we can all celebrate appropriately (see "Long Ceremony" below).
  - Submission deadline: October 31, 2007.
  - Self-nomination is welcomed.
  - We especially encourage nominations of effective use of COIN-OR that the community may not know about.

Submissions will be judged by a committee of members of the INFORMS Conference program committee and the COIN-OR Foundation, Inc.

**Long Ceremony**: The actual awarding of the COIN-OR INFORMS Cup will take place during a long ceremony at a congenial locale where cups are welcome (see below). All entrants and their supporters are welcome to join in the celebration and regale (rile) the prize winners.

  - Day: Monday, November 5
  - Time: from 9:00 - 11:00
  - Resturant: [The Seattle Cheesecake Factory (206) 652-5400](http://www.thecheesecakefactory.com/locations_state.asp?st=WA)
  - Location: 700 Pike Street, Seattle, WA 98109 (one block from the convention center)
  - Directions: [From WA State Convention Center to Cheesecake Factory](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=d&hl=en&geocode=&saddr=800+Convention+Pl,+Seattle,+WA&daddr=700+Pike+St,+Seattle,+WA+&sll=47.794245,-122.175522&sspn=0.268468,0.503311&ie=UTF8&z=19&om=1)
  - [The Bar Menu:](http://www.thecheesecakefactory.com/menu_Display.asp?pg=7)

The winner will be posted on the COIN-OR website.

Please e-mail your submissions to [coin-cup](mailto:coin-cup@coin-or.org)

Enter, Come, **possibly win**, and raise your cup with us \!
