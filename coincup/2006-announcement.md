INFORMS Annual Meeting 2006

Announcing the second annual "COIN-OR INFORMS Cup" competition --- the most coveted prize in computational OR\!

**Rules:**

  - The submission or nomination must contain a (i) synopsis of an effective use of COIN-OR or valuable contribution to COIN-OR and (ii) a cover letter explaining the significance.
  - **The winner must accept the prize in person at the 2006 INFORMS meeting** --- so we can all celebrate appropriately (see "Long Ceremony" below).
  - Submission deadline: October 31, 2006.
  - Self-nomination is welcomed.
  - We especially encourage nominations of effective use of COIN-OR that the community may not know about.

Submissions will be judged by a committee of members of the INFORMS Conference program committee and the COIN-OR Foundation, Inc.

**Long Ceremony**: The actual awarding of the COIN-OR INFORMS Cup will take place during a long ceremony at a congenial locale where cups are needed. All entrants and their supporters are welcome to join in the celebration and regale (rile) the prize winners.

  - Day: Sunday, November 5, 2006
  - Time: 9:00 - 10:30 pm
  - Location: [August Henry's](http://www.augusthenrys.com/), 946 Penn Avenue, Pittsburgh (right across the street from the Courtyard Marriott, and just half a block from the Convention Center and the Westin)

The winner will be posted on the COIN-OR website.

**Come on -- enter and win\!** Please send submissions to [coin-cup](mailto:coin-cup@coin-or.org) --- see you at the cup-raising\!
