The Strategic Leadership Board (SLB) conducts the business affairs of the Foundation, sets the overall strategic direction of the Foundation, and functions as the legal Board of Directors.

The Strategic Leadership Board consists of between 3 and 10 members, with members being elected to three-year terms from 1 January to 31 December (see the [COIN-OR Foundation by-laws](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/COIN_OR_Bylaws.pdf)).
